How it works

Back on solid ground without the damage

A winch-out is for a vehicle that runs but can't get itself out: tires buried in beach sand, sunk in mud or a soft shoulder, dropped into a ditch, or hung up off the edge of a driveway or dirt lot. We position the truck on firm ground, attach to a proper recovery point on your vehicle, and pull it out slowly and straight.

Going slow matters. Yanking a stuck car out by the bumper is how bumpers, splash shields and suspension parts get torn off. We take the extra minutes to rig it right.

Not sure whether you need a winch-out or a tow? Call and describe where the car is sitting — we'll tell you on the phone.

What to do while you wait

  1. Stop spinning the tires — it digs the car in deeper.
  2. Turn on your hazard lights and stay out of traffic.
  3. Call (714) 584-5600 with your location, the vehicle, and how it's stuck (sand, mud, ditch, curb).
  4. We quote you on the phone and give you an honest arrival time.

$150 covers the first 30 minutes, or $180 between 10pm and 6am. If the recovery will run past 30 minutes, we tell you on scene before continuing.

My car is stuck in sand at the beach. Can you get it out?

Yes. Sand is one of the most common winch-outs we do. Stop spinning the tires and call us with your location.

We can tow it. The tow is priced at the flat rate for your city, with the first three miles included and $5 per mile after.
