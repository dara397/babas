How it works

Built for low, expensive and fragile

Low front splitters, carbon aero, wide tires and long overhangs don't survive a careless hook-up. We lift exotic and low cars on wheel dollies so nothing drags, and secure the wheels with soft straps and padding so nothing scratches the rims.

You can see the work in our photos: a Lamborghini loaded at night, a Porsche 911 GT3 RS on dollies, a Ferrari wheel strapped and padded, a widebody Charger lifted clean. Same owner-operator on every job.

Moving a car to a dealership, a detail shop, a storage garage or a new home? We do point-to-point transport too.

How we handle an exotic tow

  1. You tell us the car, where it is, and where it's going — we quote on the phone.
  2. We check clearance and the best way to lift it before touching it.
  3. Wheels go on dollies; soft straps and padding secure them.
  4. We deliver it and you check it over before we leave.
